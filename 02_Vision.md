# 02_Vision.md — Genesis Vision

## The one-line vision
**A living model of the world's knowledge — ask anything, watch a world emerge.**

## What Genesis is not
- Not a search engine (search retrieves; Genesis *builds*).
- Not a chatbot (chat answers; Genesis *shows*).
- Not a knowledge graph (graphs are static; Genesis is *alive*).

## Philosophy
Knowledge is not a list of facts. It is a living system — entities appear, connect, grow,
fade, and reshape each other over time. Today's tools freeze that system into pages and
paragraphs. Genesis restores its motion: you don't read about a field, you *watch* it exist.

## The wow moment (the demo that sells it)
The judge types "Artificial Intelligence". Two seconds of nothing. Then the screen builds
itself — a glowing sphere, nodes growing outward: companies, researchers, universities,
products, funding. It feels like watching a civilization being born. No other hackathon
entry will look like this.

## Design principles
1. **Emergence over lists** — information appears spatially, growing from a seed.
2. **Evidence over magic** — every node and edge cites its sources; the Reality Meter
   always shows how current and well-supported something is.
3. **Time is a dimension** — the past is explorable (time machine), the future is
   simulatable (clearly labeled as fiction).
4. **Curiosity is the interface** — the system should feel curious, not just responsive.

## Long-term vision (beyond the hackathon)
- Any-topic world generation (healthcare, EVs, climate, finance…).
- Browser extension: generate a world for the page you're reading.
- World comparison, replay, and shared exploration trails.
- The trust layer: a Reality Meter for everything on the internet.

## What v1 proves
That a living, explorable, evidence-backed knowledge universe can be generated from live
search data in one domain — and that watching it emerge changes how people understand a field.

## On Continuous Evolution (DNA Principle 5)
"Knowledge is never complete. The world continuously grows." v1 honors this within the
credit budget: the time machine grows the world across snapshots, capped live expansion
grows it per session, and the world file is refreshable via a rebuild script. Fully
autonomous expansion is deferred to v2 — not from lack of belief, but because the SerpApi
free tier cannot fund infinity. The principle stands; the mechanism waits for budget.
